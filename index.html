Three value pillars

Translate technical data into business decisions.

Foresight · The differentiator

See what's coming.

Project component life limits and shop visit forecasts 12 to 24 months forward. Know your cost exposure before it becomes an operational constraint.

Visibility · The foundation

One screen. Entire portfolio.

Your entire fleet on one dashboard, current as of your last records update. Airframe, engines, APU, landing gear, ADs — all in one place.

Readiness · The outcome

Arrive prepared.

Know your compliance and airworthiness posture before your next audit or redelivery. No last-minute surprises. No exposure you didn't see coming.

The cost of not knowing

Unplanned events don't announce themselves.

Redelivery disputes

Conditions found at aircraft return that weren't tracked forward become negotiating leverage for the other side. Every discrepancy discovered at redelivery is a position already lost.

↑ Fletaris projects redelivery exposure 12–24 months out
Unplanned shop visits

The premium on an unplanned engine or APU shop visit versus a scheduled one is significant — in cost, in timing, and in aircraft availability. Visibility over the forward window eliminates the surprise.

↑ Fletaris forecasts shop visits against the 12-month window
Compliance gaps at audit

An airworthiness gap found by an auditor instead of found by you comes with a deadline, a cost, and a counterparty watching. Being prepared is a posture, not a document.

↑ Fletaris monitors compliance posture in real time
